The Middle Ages
The period after the Norman Conquest is known as the Middle Ages. William the Conqueror became king of England after defeating Harold at the Battle of Hastings in 1066.
The Domesday Book
William sent people all over England to compile a record of land ownership. This was called the Domesday Book, and it still survives today as an invaluable historical document.
"The Domesday Book listed every town and village, who owned them, how many people lived there, and what animals they had."
Magna Carta
In 1215, King John was forced to agree to a charter of rights called Magna Carta. This established that even the king was subject to the law.
  • Established the rule of law in England
  • Limited the power of the monarchy
  • Protected the rights of the nobility
